Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Unicorn Scout is a personal project: a chatbot that answers questions about India's unicorn startups using a public Kaggle dataset. This page explains exactly what it stores and why.

What we collect

  • Your messages and the bot's answers, with timestamps, so your chats appear in the sidebar and the bot can follow up on earlier questions.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account id. We ask for nothing else: no contacts, no Drive, no Gmail.
  • If you don't sign in: a random guest id in a cookie, so your chats survive a page reload.
  • Technical data about each answer: which steps ran, how long they took, how many tokens were used and whether an error occurred. This is used to monitor quality and fix problems.

Where it goes

  • Chats, accounts and logs are stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon.
  • To answer, your message is sent to the AI model providers Groq and Google (Gemini). On Google's free tier, prompts may be used by Google to improve its products, so please don't type personal or confidential information.
  • Answer traces (your message, the answer and timing) are recorded in Langfuse for debugging. Traces carry an anonymous user id, not your name or email.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone else.

Cookies

One signed, HttpOnly cookie (scout_session) keeps you signed in or remembers your guest chats for up to 30 days. Your theme preference is kept in your browser's local storage. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

Deleting a chat removes it from your sidebar. To have your account and all your chats permanently erased, email knandan400@gmail.com and it will be done within 30 days. Signing out clears the cookie from your browser.

Contact

Questions about this policy: knandan400@gmail.com.
